Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PLM Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Rhodes & Rain Records. It is used only with that owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload and update the channel's own videos, and the YouTube Analytics API to read that channel's own viewing statistics.

How data is stored

The sign-in token and the channel statistics are kept on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/riperdy-tech/plm-publisher.
